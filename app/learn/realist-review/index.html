Evidence synthesis / Method

Realist review

How does this programme work, for whom, and in which contexts?

Synthesize mixed evidence to explain how complex programmes work: for whom, in what contexts, and why.

How it works

  1. Ask how

    Do not ask only whether a programme works. Ask for whom, in which circumstances, and why.

    “The realist research question is often summarized as "What works for whom under what circumstances, how and why?"”

    Wong et al. (2013), Background, What is a realist synthesis?

  2. Draft a theory

    Write down the programme theory: the ideas its designers had about how it should produce its outcomes.

    “reviewers may begin by eliciting from the literature the main ideas that went into the making of a class of interventions (the program theory). This program theory sets out how and why a class of intervention is thought to 'work'”

    Wong et al. (2013), Background, What is a realist synthesis?

  3. Search as you go

    Search for any kind of evidence that can test the theory, across disciplines, and search again as the theory changes.

    “Searching should be guided by the objectives and focus of the synthesis, and revised iteratively in the light of emerging data.”

    Wong et al. (2013), Item 8: Searching process, explanation

  4. Judge each piece

    Judge each piece of data, not whole studies: does it help build or test the theory, and was it produced credibly?

    “Relevance - whether it can contribute to theory building and/or testing; and”

    Wong et al. (2013), Item 9: Selection and appraisal of documents, explanation

    “Rigor - whether the method used to generate that particular piece of data is credible and trustworthy.”

    Wong et al. (2013), Item 9: Selection and appraisal of documents, explanation

  5. Find CMO patterns

    Look for recurring patterns: in this context, this mechanism fires and produces this outcome.

    “realism adheres to a generative explanation for causation - that is, an outcome (O) of interest was generated by relevant mechanism(s) (M) being triggered in context (C).”

    Wong et al. (2013), Item 11: Analysis and synthesis processes, explanation

  6. Refine the theory

    Test the theory against the patterns, change it, and test again. The result is a refined explanation, not a pooled effect.

    “The key analytic process in realist review involves iterative testing and refinement of theoretically based explanations using empirical findings in data sources.”

    Wong et al. (2013), Item 11: Analysis and synthesis processes, explanation

Where it breaks, and what protects it

  • Researcher bias

    You see what you expected to see.

    Write down your own lens, and let a second coder check.

  • Apples & oranges

    Pooling apples with oranges.

    Plan in advance how you'll explain why studies differ.

A real example

Pawson's realist syntheses of complex social programmes (context + mechanism → outcome).

Defining reference: Pawson et al. (2005). Realist review - a new method of systematic review designed for complex policy interventions. Journal of Health Services Research & Policy.
