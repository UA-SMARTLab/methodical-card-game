Duel

Each of you is dealt 7 Design cards and 3 tactics cards (Threats and Safeguards). Three research questions lie face up. Every round you both play one design, face down, onto any open question. Then the cards flip.

  • Score it: best +3, defensible +1, unlisted 0, trap −2. Answer the same question as your rival with a better design and you scoop them: +1 more.
  • Review it: play a Threat your rival's design is weak against. They lose 1, unless they block it with a Safeguard that counters it (then they gain 1).
  • Plan ahead: once per round you may swap a design for a new one (−1). Hands refill after every round. Highest score after 8 rounds wins.
