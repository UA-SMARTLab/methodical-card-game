You versus the computer

Duel

Each of you is dealt 7 Design cards and 3 tactics cards (Threats and Safeguards). Four research questions lie face up. Every round you both play one design, face down, onto any open question. Then the cards flip.

  • Score it: best +3, defensible +1, unlisted 0, trap −2. Answer the same question as your rival with a better design and you scoop them: +1 more.
  • Review it: challenge your rival's design by naming one of its weaknesses: +1 to you and −1 to them if it lands, −1 to you if it isn't a real weakness. They can rebut by naming a Safeguard that counters it and suits their design, and then they gain 1 instead. A challenge is worth it against weak defenders and costly against strong ones. A matching Threat or Safeguard card in hand makes it automatic.
  • Draft: after each round, take one of four face-up designs into your hand. Pick the one that answers a question still on the table.
  • Plan ahead: once per round you may swap a design for a random new one (−1). Highest score after 8 rounds wins.
