# Methodical: German terminology (Glossar)

Why the German card text uses the terms it does. Written for a native methodologist who wants to check or overrule a choice.
Review of 30 October 2026, done against the English source in `i18n/en/`.

**Rule used.** Where German-speaking researchers really use the English term (in IQWiG, DNEbM or Cochrane texts, or in the
German Wikipedia article for the method), the English term stays, spelled the German way: nouns capitalised and
multi-word compounds hyphenated (Duden rule for English loan compounds), e.g. *Immortal-Time-Bias*, *Target-Trial-Emulation*.
Where an established German term exists, it is used. Grammatical gender of *Review* is **masculine** throughout
(*der systematische Review*, *ein Scoping Review*), following the DNEbM glossary.

**Card type.** The card type is *Methode* (deck *Methoden*), following the English *Method*: *Design* on its own reads as
styling in German. *Studiendesign* stays the technical term for a study design in running text.
